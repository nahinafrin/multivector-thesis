8. SUBSTITUTIONS AND CAVEATS (state these alongside the numbers)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
huggingface.co is blocked by network policy on both machines, so the draft's models could not be used:
  - Sentinel embedder BAAI/bge-m3 -> LSA (TF-IDF + 256-d SVD) fitted on kb_wiki.texts.
  - ControlNET probe model all-MiniLM-L6-v2 -> RoBERTa-base inside spaCy en_core_web_trf 3.8.0
    (GitHub release), hidden_states[-4] mean-pooled. Neither is the paper's own backbone (Llama3/
    Vicuna/Mistral for ControlNET), so absolute numbers are indicative, not a reproduction.
Context for both baselines = first 5 chunks the thesis pipeline actually retrieved (FAISS/bge-m3) +
  the injected poison chunk. 38 query-attack rows had no retrieval in the pipeline log (blocked at
  the gate); for those the baselines used top-5 LSA chunks from kb_wiki.texts.
Sentinel cue-phrase list: 6 of its 14 patterns mirror this slice's own poison wording (editorial
  note, authoritative source, ...). Section 4 'generic cue phrases only' removes them — that row is
  the fairer estimate of Sentinel's performance on unseen attack wording.
ControlNET's probe is trained on prompts only (train/val carry no retrieved context) and then applied
  to query+context inputs. That mismatch pushes every context-bearing input over threshold (draft-train
  row = 100%/100%, a degenerate result). The 'ctx-matched' rows train on prompt+KB-context inputs; the
  max-F1 threshold is still degenerate (val is ~83% unsafe), so 'ctx-matched, tau@5%FPR' is the fairest
  ControlNET operating point. The probe never sees poisoned contexts in training.
Max-F1 threshold rule is the draft's (and C3RF's) rule; on an 83%-unsafe val split it favours
  near-always-block thresholds for both ControlNET variants — hence the tau@5%FPR rows.
Full-pipeline numbers are from the existing run step4 dataset/baseline_comparison/grounded_full.jsonl
  (not re-run here; it needs Ollama). Its benign FPR includes blocks by the risk-feedback controller.
